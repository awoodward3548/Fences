Put product photos here to show them in the Fence Builder style gallery.

Name each file after the product line and style, joined by two underscores:
  montage_plus__majestic.jpg
  wood_privacy__shadowbox.png
or after the style alone, to use it for every line that has the style:
  invincible.jpg

The ids for lines and styles are in data/catalog.json.
Supported formats: .jpg .jpeg .png .gif .webp
Styles without a photo show a drawing generated from the current settings.
